Tapped.one Transparency

How We Rank

A transparent look at how Tapped.one collects information, reviews providers and creates community ratings.

01

Where We Get Information

Information can come from multiple sources. We don't rely on a single person's opinion when reviewing a provider.

Community feedback
User reports
Provider and community servers
Publicly available information
Research and testing where possible
Historical reports and feedback
02

Our Review Process

Information is reviewed and compared before it is used to influence a provider's rating.

Information is collected
Reports are reviewed
Multiple opinions are considered
Conflicting information is investigated
Ratings can be reviewed again
03

How A Rating Is Reached

Our goal is to have information reviewed by at least 3 people. Their findings are compared until an agreement or reasonable consensus is reached.

INFORMATION Reports & research
→
3 REVIEWERS Independent opinions
→
AGREEMENT Compare findings
→
RATING Updated when needed
04

What We Look At

Different factors can contribute to a provider's overall rating depending on the category and available information.

Security Reported security concerns and available evidence.
Reliability Stability and consistency based on available feedback.
Performance Performance reports and user experiences.
Updates Maintenance, changes and update history.
Community Feedback Patterns in feedback received from users.
Reported Issues Repeated or significant concerns reported to us.
05

AI-Assisted Analysis

AI may be used to help organize, compare and analyze information that we receive.

AI DOES NOT REPLACE HUMAN REVIEW

AI can assist with identifying patterns and organizing information, but it does not independently decide whether a provider is trustworthy, legitimate or safe.

06

Ratings Can Change

A rating isn't permanent. New information can cause a provider to be reviewed again.

New evidence
New community reports
Provider changes
New testing or research
!

Important Information

Reports received from users or community servers may be incomplete, inaccurate or conflicting. A report is not automatically treated as a confirmed fact.

Tapped.one attempts to consider the available information and its credibility before using it in our rankings. However, we cannot independently verify every claim.
